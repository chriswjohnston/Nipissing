THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP OF NIPISSING

BY-LAW NUMBER 2026-41

Being a By-Law to appoint Fence Viewers for the Township of
Nipissing.

WHEREAS Section 227 of the Municipal Act, S.O. 2001, Chapter 25, authorizes Councils to
appoint such officers as may be necessary for the purposes of the Corporation;

AND WHEREAS the Line Fences Act, R.S.O. 1990, c.L.17, s.2 states that the Council of every
local Municipality shall by by-law appoint such number of fence viewers as are required to carry
out the provisions of the Act;

NOW THEREFORE THE COUNCIL OF THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP OF
NIPISSING ENACTS AS FOLLOWS:

1) That Dan MacInnis, and are hereby
appointed as Fence Viewers for the Township of Nipissing.

2) That each Fence Viewer shall be paid at the base labour rate per hour for work performed
under the Line Fences Act;

3) This By-Law shall come into force and take effect on the date it receives final passing.
By-Law Number 2022-50 is hereby rescinded.

READ A FIRST, SECOND AND THIRD TIME AND PASSED THIS 17™ DAY OF
NOVEMBER, 2026.
