Corporation of the Township of Nipissing
3950 Highway 522, Concession A, Lot 174, Plan 42R5662, Part 3, 4 and 5

BY-LAW NO. 2026-16
“Being a By-law to Amend Zoning By-law 2020-20, as amended”

WHEREAS the Corporation of the Township of Nipissing has received an application to
amend Zoning By-law 2020-20 as amended;

AND WHEREAS Section 34 of the Planning Act, R.S.O. 1990, c.P.13, as amended,
authorizes the Council of a municipality to pass Zoning By-laws to regulate the use of
land, buildings or structures in accordance with Section 34(1) of the Planning Act
Planning Act, R.S.0. 1990, c.P.13.

NOW THEREFORE the Council of the Corporation of the Township of Nipissing enacts
that By-law 2020-20, as amended, is hereby amended as follows:

1. That the subject property at 3950 Highway 522, Concession A, Lot 174, Plan
42R5662, Part 3, 4 and 5, be rezoned from the Residential Hamlet (HR) Zone
to the Special Zone No. 25 (SZ-25) Zone as shown on Schedule ‘A1’; and,

2. That Schedule A to Zoning By-law 2020-20 shall be amended in accordance
with Schedule ‘A1’, attached hereto.

3. That Section 5.25 shall be added following Section 5.24. Section 5.25 shall
read _as follows:

“5.25 SPECIAL ZONE NO. 25 (3950 Highway 522)

Notwithstanding any other provision of this By-law on Concession A, Lot 174,
Plan 42R5662, Part 3, 4 and 5, in the Township of Nipissing being 3950
Highway 522, the following applies:

5.25.1 Zone Boundaries

The zone boundaries of the Special Zone No. 25 (SZ-25) are identified on the
Schedules to this By-law.

5.25.2 Permitted Uses


In addition to the permitted uses in the Residential Hamlet (RH) Zone, a Food
Stand shall also be a permitted use.

Read a first, second and third time, and passed this 21%t day of April, 2026.
