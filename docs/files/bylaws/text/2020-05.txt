THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP OF NIPISSING 
BY-LAW NUMBER 2020-05 
Being a By-Law to limit the gross weight of any vehicle or any 
class thereof passing over a bridge under the jurisdiction of the 
Corporation of the Township ofNipissing pursuant to the Highway 
Traffic Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. H. 8, as amended. 
WHEREAS Section 123(2) of the Highway Traffic Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. H. 8, as amended 
provides that a municipal corporation or other authority having jurisdiction over a bridge may by 
by-law limit the gross vehicle weight of any vehicle or any class thereof passing over the bridge; 
AND WHEREAS the Council of the Corporation of the Township ofNipissing deems it 
advisable to enact a by-law to limit the gross weight of any class thereof passing over the bridge 
under the jurisdiction of the Corporation of the Township ofNipissing; 
NOW THEREFORE the Council of the Township ofNipissing ENACTS AS FOLLOWS: 
1. That By-Law 2019-42, Section 2 is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the 
following: 
2. No vehicle or combination of any class thereof with a gross vehicle weight in 
excess of L 1 = 3 3 tonnes, L2 = 23 tonnes, L3 = 1 7 tonnes shall be operated over 
Bridge No. 6-Hummel Bridge located at Lot 1, Concession 2 on the boundary 
road known as Hemlock Road between the Township of Ni pissing and the 
Municipality of Powassan. 
READ A FIRST, SECOND AND THIRD TIME AND PASSED THIS 21 ST DAY OF 
JANUARY, 2020.
