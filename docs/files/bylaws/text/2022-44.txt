Corporation of the Township of Nipissing
2675 Highway 654

BY-LAW NO. 2022 - 44

“Being a By-law to Amend Zoning By-law 2020-20 for the
Removal of a Holding (H) Symbol, as amended”

WHEREAS the Corporation of the Township of Nipissing has received an application to
amend Zoning By-law 2020-20 for the Removal of a Holding (H1) Symbol, as amended;

AND WHEREAS Section 36 of the Planning Act, R.S.O. 1990, c.P.13, as amended, applies
to the Removal of a Holding (H1) Symbol;

NOW THEREFORE the Council of the Corporation of the Township of Nipissing enacts
that By-law 2020-20, as amended is hereby amended as follows:

1. That the Holding (H1) Symbol shall be removed on the subject lands located
at 2675 Highway 654 in accordance with Schedule A1 to By-law No. 2022 - 44,
attached hereto.

Read a first, second and third time and passed this 20"" day of September, 2022.

Tom Lip Mayor

Municipal Administrator-Clerk-Treasurer
