THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP OF NIPISSING
BY-LAW NUMBER 2026-26

Being a by-law to Amend By-Law Number 2022-34, cited as the “Procedural By-Law” to update the
public notice guidelines for meetings.

WHEREAS the Township of Nipissing Council passed By-Law Number 2022-34, the Procedural By-

Law, to establish rules governing the proceedings of Council, the calling of meetings and the
conduct of Members pursuant to Section 238 of the Municipal Act, 2001;

AND WHEREAS changes to the to the Procedural By-Law will provide clarity on certain items in the
By-Law;

NOW THEREFORE the Council of the Corporation of the Township of Nipissing amends By-Law
Number 2022-34 to the following:

1.18 Municipal Administrator means the person appointed by the Township and assumes duties
pursuant to Section 229 (Chief Administrative Officer) of the Municipal Act and other relevant

legislation. <numbering amendments to definitions to follow this inclusion>

Effective Date:
That this By-law shall come into force and effect immediately on the passing thereof.

READ A FIRST, SECOND AND THIRD TIME AND PASSED THIS 16'" DAY OF JUNE, 2026.
