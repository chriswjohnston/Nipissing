NIPISSING 
To provide a guideline for the use of a trailer/cottage as a Temporary Residence 
during construction of a Permanent Residence. 
Scope: 
Township of Nipissing. 
Policy: 
As the use of trailers/cottages as a Temporary Residence during the 
construction of a Permanent Residence is not permitted under the Zoning By-
law, a request may be submitted to allow the temporary use. 
The following conditions must be met: 
1. A $5,000.00 Letter of Credit shall be deposited with the Municipality. This 
is to guarantee that the trailer/cottage will be removed and not used as a 
residence after the occurrence of the first of the following events: 
a. 30 days after the CBO confirms that the Permanent Residence is 
ready for occupancy; 
b. 30 days after occupancy of the Permanent Residence; 
c. In any event such use of the trailer/cottage as a Temporary 
Residence is to terminate on a date specified at the time of 
issuance of the Building Permit. 
2. That the form of the letter shall be prior approved by the CAO-Clerk. 
Failure to comply with these conditions will result in the Letter of Credit being 
cashed and the monies therefrom shall be used by the Township to proceed with 
legal action to have the trailer/cottage removed. 
Attached as Schedule "B" is a sample letter as prepared by the Township 
Solicitor to be used as a guide when dealing with these requests.
