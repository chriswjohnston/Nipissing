THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP OF NIPISSING

BY-LAW NUMBER 2025-38

Being a By-Law to confirm the appointment of members to
the Township of Nipissing Committee of Adjustment.

WHEREAS the Township of Nipissing has passed a by-law under section 34 of the
Planning Act, R.S.O. 1990;

AND WHEREAS the Council of the Township of Nipissing may by by-law constitute and
appoint a committee of adjustment for the municipality composed of such persons, not
fewer than three, as the council considers advisable under section c. P. 13, s. 44(1) of
the Planning Act, R.S.O.

NOW THEREFORE the Council of the Township of Nipissing enacts as follows:

That the following persons shall constitute and are hereby appointed as members of the
Committee of Adjustment:

Dave Yemm to hold office until November 17, 2026.
Lisa Chalapenko to hold office until November 17, 2026.
Shelly Foote to hold office until November 17, 2026.
Stephen Kirkey to hold office until November 17, 2026.
James Scott to hold office until November 17, 2026.
Penelope Wallace to hold office until December 1, 2026.
Paul Lafrance to hold office until December 1, 2026.

NOUR WN

By-Law Number 2025-01 is hereby rescinded.
READ A FIRST, SECOND AND THIRD TIME AND PASSED THIS 1674 DAY OF DECEMBER,
2025.

THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP
OF NIPISSING
