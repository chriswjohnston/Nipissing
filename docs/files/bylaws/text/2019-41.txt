THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP OF NIPISSING 
BY-LAW NUMBER 2019-41 
Being a By-Law to adopt Minimum Maintenance Standards for Municipal Highways 
WHEREAS Section 44(1) of the Municipal Act, R.S.O. 2001, states that municipalities shall 
keep all highways and bridges under its jurisdiction in a state of repair that is reasonable in the 
circumstances including the character and location of the highway or bridge; 
AND WHEREAS Section 44(4) of the Municipal Act, R.S.O. 2001, states that the Minister of 
Transportation may make regulations establishing minimum standards of repair for highways 
and bridges or any class of them; 
AND WHEREAS the Minister has done so in Ontario Regulation 239/02 Minimum 
Maintenance Standards for Municipal Highways (MMS), by prescribing standards for Classes 1 
through 6 highways, based on the speed limit applicable to it and the average daily traffic on it; 
AND WHEREAS the Township ofNipissing passed By-Law Number 2013-39 authorizing the 
speed limit of sixty ( 60) kilometers per hour for all unpasted roads under its jurisdiction; 
NOW THEREFORE the Council of The Corporation of the Township ofNipissing enacts as 
follows: 
Part 1 - Definitions 
CITATION AND SCHEDULES: This By-Law shall be cited as the "Township of Ni pissing 
Road Status and Maintenance By-Law". 
Schedule A, being Ontario Regulation 239/02 and any amendment thereto, is hereby 
incorporated by reference into this By-Law. 
Schedule B showing Public Roads, average annual daily vehicle counts and the Class of various 
roads is annexed to and forms part of this By-Law. 
Schedule C being the Winter Operation Plan and Deployment Procedure for the Township of 
Nipissing, annexed to and forms part of this By-Law. 
Schedule D being the Winter Patrol Procedures and Winter Patrol Records for the South and 
North Roads, annexed to and forms part of this By-Law. 
Schedule E being the Winter Operations Plow Routes for the Township ofNipissing, annexed to 
and forms part of this By-Law. 
Schedule F being the Winter Patrol roads for the Township ofNipissing, annexed to and forms 
part of this By-Law. 
1.1 
Public Road - means a "highway" within the meaning of the Municipal Act of Ontario, 
2001, as amended, which is under the jurisdiction of the Township ofNipissing. 
1.2 
Public Road Allowance - means an allowance for a road surveyed by the original 
Crown surveyors of the Township or subsequently conveyed and dedicated as a Public 
Road that is located within the Township ofNipissing and which is recorded in the 
records of the Land Registry Office as being owned by the Corporation of the Township 
ofNipissing or the municipality having jurisdiction over such road allowance. 
1.3 
Private Road - means a road that is neither owned nor maintained by the municipality. 
1.4 
Significant Weather Event- means an approaching or occurring weather hazard with 
the potential to pose a significant danger to users of the highways within a municipality. 
1.5 
Weather Hazard - means the weather hazards determined by Environment Canada as 
meeting the criteria for the issuance of an alert under its Public Weather Alerting 
Program.

2.1 
ROAD CLASSIFICATION: Public Roads within the Township shall be classified as 
per Schedule A, based on the following two factors: 
a. 
Maximum speed limit - as defined by the Township's Speed Limit on Municipal 
Roads By-Law 2013-39. 
b. 
Vehicle Use - as defined by the Average Annual Daily Traffic (AADT), counted 
once every five (5) years by the Township. For roads with high seasonal vehicle 
use, AADT will be counted in both the summer months and in the off-season, and 
assigned the more restrictive class based on the higher of the two traffic counts. 
2.2 
POLICY PRINCIPLES: In carrying out its responsibilities to maintain Public Roads 
and bridges, the Township will adhere to the following: 
a. 
Operational activities of the Roads Department shall be directed to maintain the 
Township's Public Roads to the prescribed standards herein. Care shall be taken 
first to assure that the standards of the MMS are maintained and secondly that 
such services are efficiently and effectively rendered. 
b. 
Neither this Township nor its officials make any promises or assurance that Public 
Roads or any Public Road in particular will be maintained in excess of the 
minimum standards herein defined now or in the future. The fact that the 
Township may, and indeed hopes, to exceed those standards, when the resources 
available permit it to do so, does not change the standards prescribed herein. 
c. 
The standards and road classifications shall be made publicly available to 
ratepayers and other users of the municipal road network. Council will review 
concerns about the standards and their implementation within a reasonable time 
when they are brought to its attention. 
d. 
Budgets and Council priorities shall be set on the basis that the minimum 
maintenance standards prescribed herein must be met. 
e. 
Where the Township is asked to upgrade the maintenance standards of a particular 
Public Road, it will generally do so on the basis that the average annual daily 
usage of the road has significantly increased and places it in a higher Class as 
prescribed in Schedule A. 
f. 
Where the Township is asked to accept a Private Road into the municipal road 
system, and agrees in principle to do so, it will generally require the proponents to 
first bring the road to specified standards as to design, roadbed, and surface, at the 
proponents' own expense.

2.4 
SIGNIFICANT WEATHER EVENT: When a Weather Hazard occurs that meets the 
criteria for the issuance of an alert under the Public Weather Alerting Program by Environment 
Canada, the Operations Superintendent or Designate shall monitor the weather. (0. Reg 239/02 
(4.1) 
The Operations Superintendent or Designate shall have the designated authority to declare a 
significant weather event and deploy the public works crew as soon as practicable with regard to 
public and staff safety. If a Significant Weather Event is declared, the Operations 
Superintendent or Designate shall ensure that a Declaration Notice to advise the public of the 
nature of the event and expected service alteration is completed and published. 
The Operations Superintendent or Designate shall continue to monitor the weather and when 
practicable, declare the end of the event with the completion of an End of Declared Notice being 
completed and published. 
For the purposes of 2.4 of this By-Law, the Declared Notice and End of Declared Notice shall be 
completed and published the following ways: 
a. Posting on the Township website, notice page. 
b. Circulation of the notice to the following local radio stations: 
CK.AT (600 AM); CKFX-FM (101.9 FM); CHUR-FM (100.5 FM) and CFXN-FM 
(106.3 FM) 
c. Circulation of the notice to the Nipissing Fire Department, Parry Sound Emergency 
Medical Services, North Bay Central Ambulance Communication Centre and the 
Ontario Provincial Police, Northeastern.

Winter Operation Plan and Deployment Procedure 
The purpose of this Plan and Procedure is to provide Public Works staff with a complete 
understanding of the Winter Operations methodology and expectations for the Township of 
Ni pissing. 
The Operations Superintendent has the authority to implement this procedure when they have 
determined it is required. In the absence of the Operations Superintendent, the Foreman will 
be deemed to have designated authority as required. 
Winter Seasons 
Shall start November 15th of each year and ends April 15th of the following year. Winter crews 
shall be on call between these months. Notwithstanding the generally understood winter 
season time frame, abnormal and severe weather events have been occurring at unexpected 
intervals and staff will be expected to respond when needed as is practicable. 
Winter Sand 
Shall be mixed with 3% salt and stacked in sand dome by August 1st to ensure time to drain 
excess moisture from the sand. 
Equipment 
The Township shall have 3 tandem plow wing sander trucks, one grader with wing tower and 
a 1-ton pick-up truck with snow plow and sander. Equipment shall be ready to go into service 
no later than November ist. 
Winter maintenance equipment may be removed from the equipment any time after March 
31st as instructed by the Operations Superintendent. 
Routes 
Each piece of equipment shall have an assigned route to plow/sand. When sand truck 
operators finish their routes, they shall assist with the sanding of the grader route as required. 
1-ton truck shall plow/sand Township Office, Fire Station 1/Community Centre/Fitness Centre 
and Fire Station 2 yards. 
Winter Operations Plow Routes map attached as Schedule E to the Township of Nipissing Road 
Status and Maintenance By-Law. Where routes will be followed, equipment may be changed 
in response to the severity of the occurrence or the nature of the work to be performed, such 
as the winging back of the snow banks. The Operations Superintendent or designate will have 
the authority to make those required changes. 
Call outs 
Winter crews shall be mobilized within an hour of call out time. 
End of shift 
Trucks and equipment shall be fueled and ready to return to service after each plow run. 
Truck and equipment shall be washed within 24 hours of last use. 
Sander chains shall be visually inspected after each run. 
Plow equipment shall be left in heated garage when not in use. 
Maintenance 
Truck and equipment shall be greased weekly and checked for any problems by each operator. 
Problems or concerns regarding trucks or equipment or the efficiency/safety of same, shall be 
reported to the Operations Superintendent immediately. In the absence of the Operations 
Superintendent, the Foreman shall be considered to have full authority and act in the 
Operations Superintendent's place.

Winter Patrol Procedures 
• 
Winter equipment shall be ready November 1st. 
• 
Winter patrol shall start November 15th and shall end April 15th . *In the event of a 
winter weather event outside of these dates, Winter Patrol Procedures shall be provided 
until road conditions are considered acceptable. 
• 
Patrol rotation shall start on Sunday and finish on Saturday of each week. 
• 
Patroller shall drive to the shop each morning and pick up patrol truck. 
• 
Patrol truck fuel tank shall never be left less than half tank of fuel. 
• 
Patroller on duty shall start patrol at 3am each day, working continuously for the 
scheduled 8 hours. *In the event of a winter weather event requiring winter 
maintenance operations, working hours may be extended past the scheduled shift as 
needed. 
• 
Patroller on weekend duty shall do their 4-hour patrol and go home if no action is 
required. 
• 
Patroller shall track time and date of all call outs on the corresponding Patrol Sheet. 
• 
Patroller shall fill out daily Patrol Sheets. The Patrol Sheets are in two parts, breaking 
the total roads into groupings of north anp south. Winter Patrol Sheets for North and 
South are attached to and form a part of this procedure. The Winter Patrol roads 
designated for North and South patrol areas are indicated on Schedule F, attached to 
and forming part of the Township of Nipissing Road Status and Maintenance By-Law. 
• 
Patroller shall patrol 10% of Township roads, with specific attention given to previously 
identified problem areas which are known to drift, have big hills and sharp corners, 
ensuring these areas are sufficiently evaluated. 
• 
When a winter weather event occurs, the Patroller on call shall activate the winter crew 
once an accumulation of 5cm of snow or sleet is reached. When a winter weather event 
continues, roads shall be plowed and sanded every 10 hours until the event ends and 
roads are acceptable. 
• 
When a winter event occurs in evening/night, the Patroller on call shall call the winter 
crew in as early as possible to begin plowing and sanding Township roads. 
• 
Operations Superintendent will print out Weather Report each morning.
