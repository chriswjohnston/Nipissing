THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP OF NIPISSING
BY-LAW NUMBER 2024-12

Being a by-law to Amend By-Law Number 2019-41, cited as the Township of Nipissing Road Status
and Maintenance By-Law.

WHEREAS the Township of Nipissing Council passed By-Law Number 2019-41, the Road Status and
Maintenance By-Law to adopt Minimum Maintenance Standards for Municipal Highways in the

Township of Nipissing;
AND WHEREAS changes to procedures, equipment and programs used are required;

NOW THEREFORE the Council of the Corporation of the Township of Nipissing amends By-Law
Number 2019-41 for the following:

2.4(a) Posting on the Township website, social media pages in use and CodeRed.

2.4(b) Delete.

Schedule C, Winter Seasons — To be added after “Winter crews shall be on cail between these

months.”:
Patrol may be suspended or re-scheduled in accordance with weather requirements by the

Operations Superintendent or designate.

Schedule D, Winter Patrol Procedures:

Point #3 — change to “Patrol rotation shall start on Thursday and finish on Wednesday each week.”
Point #6 — change to “Patroller on duty shall start patrol at or before 4 am in response to weather
conditions. The Operations Superintendent or designate may alter patrol start times at their
discretion dependent on weather conditions, current and anticipated. *In the event of a winter

weather event requiring winter maintenance operations, working hours may be extended past the
scheduled shift as needed.”

Point #9 — Replace three (3) instances of “Patrol Sheets” with “Patrol Tablet”.

Point #13 — change to “Weather conditions will be completed each day by entering information
into Patrol Tablet using the weather app in use. To be completed by the Patroller on duty.”

Effective Date:
That this By-law shall come into force and effect immediately on the passing thereof.
