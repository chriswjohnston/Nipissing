BY-LAW NO. 2026 - 25

“Being a By-law to Amend Zoning By-law 2020-20, as amended”

WHEREAS the Corporation of the Township of Nipissing has received an application to
amend Zoning By-law 2020-20 as amended;

AND WHEREAS Section 34 of the Planning Act, R.S.O. 1990, c.P.13, as amended,
authorizes the Council of a municipality to Pass Zoning By-laws to regulate the use of
land, buildings or structures in accordance with Section 34(1) of the Planning Act
Planning Act, R.S.O. 1990, c.P.13.

NOW THEREFORE the Council of the Corporation of the Township of Nipissing enacts
that By-law 2020-20, as amended, is hereby amended as follows:

1, That the property located at 79 Hinchberger Bay Road, Part Lot 9, Concession
19, be rezoned from the Open Space (OS) Zone to the Shoreline Residential
(RS) Zone, as shown on Schedule ‘A1’;

2. That the property located at 95 Hinchberger Bay Road, Part Lot 9, Concession
19, be rezoned from the Shoreline Residential (SR) Zone to the Special Zone
No. 26 (SZ 26), as shown on Schedule ‘A1’;

3. That Section 5.26 be added in accordance with the following:

5.26

5.26.1

5.26.2

5.26.3

SPECIAL ZONE NO. 26 (95 Hinchberger Bay Road)
Notwithstanding the other provisions of this By-law, on Part
Lot 9, Concession 19, being 95 Hinchberger Bay Road, the
following applies:

Zone Boundaries

The zone boundaries of Special Zone No. 26 (SZ 26) are
identified on the Schedules of this By-law.

Permitted Uses

The permitted uses and regulations of the Shoreline
Residential (SR) Zone shalt apply to Special Zone No. 26 (SZ
26).

e Minimum Lot Area: 4,400 square metres.

4. Schedule A to Zoning By-law 2020-20 shalt be amended in accordance with
Schedule ‘A1’, attached hereto.

Read a first, second and third time and passed this 16th of June, 2026.
