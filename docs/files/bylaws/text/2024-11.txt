THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP OF NIPISSING
BY-LAW NUMBER 2024-11

Being a by-law to Amend By-Law Number 2021-50, to regulate the parking of vehicles within the
Corporation of the Township of Nipissing.

WHEREAS the Township of Nipissing Council passed By-Law Number 2021-50, the Parking By-Law,
to establish rules to regulate the parking of vehicles;

AND WHEREAS changes to property boundaries and parking restrictions are required;

NOW THEREFORE the Council of the Corporation of the Township of Nipissing amends By-Law
Number 2024-11 for the following:

2.3(6) Remove.
2.5 Remove (“Party Rock”).
7.6 Remove “his/her” replace with “their”.

Schedule A, 1. Remove “Unopened Road Allowance between Concession 18 and 19, Lot 11 and the
abutting Shore Road Allowance — designated as an Emergency Loading Zone, no parking at any
time. Update to Illustration 1 as required.

Schedule A, 3. Change to “Birchgrove Drive up to intersection with Lakeview Drive; Rocky Shore
Drive up to intersection with Pike Bay Road and Sunset Cove Road up to intersection with Sandy
Bay Road — No parking on roadway. (Reference Illustration 1).

Schedule A, 10. Remove entirely — now included in 3.

Iustration 1, update to restrict parking along both sides of the road along Birchgrove Drive up to
the intersection with Lakeview Drive; Rocky Shore Drive up to the intersection with Pike Bay Road
and Sunset Cove Road up to the intersection with Sandy Bay Road.

Illustration 8, remove illustration.

Effective Date:
That this By-law shall come into force and effect immediately on the passing thereof.
