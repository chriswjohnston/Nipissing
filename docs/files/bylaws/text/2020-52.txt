THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP OF NIPISSING 
BY-LAW NUMBER 2020-52 
Being a By-Law to amend Schedule E to By-Law 2019-41, Minimum 
Maintenance Standards, Plow Routes. 
WHEREAS the Council of the Corporation of the Township ofNipissing passed By-Law 2019-
41 adopting the Minimum Maintenance Standards for Municipal Highways; 
AND WHEREAS the Council of the Corporation of the Township ofNipissing deems it 
necessary to amend Schedule E to By-Law 2019-41 to remove the truck numbers from specific 
plow routes in order to efficiently respond to winter weather events as required. 
NOW THEREFORE THE COUNCIL OF THE CORPORATION OF THE TOWNSHIP OF 
NIPISSING ENACTS AS FOLLOWS: 
1. 
That Schedule E to By-Law 2019-41 be amended, attached to and forming part of this 
By-Law as Schedule E. 
2. 
That this By-law will come into effect upon passing. 
By-law 2019-41 is hereby amended. 
READ A FIRST, SECOND AND THIRD TIME AND PASSED THIS 15TH DAY OF 
DECEMBER, 2020.
