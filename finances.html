      Documents

      Every financial document, by year

      Checked every day: the township’s financial pages, its media library and every meeting’s documents. Anything new is copied the day it appears. For documents the township removed before a copy was kept, web archives are searched daily; if you have one, add it to the inbox.

      Where the numbers come from

      The Financial Information Return each municipality files with the Province, built from its audited statements, on the same form every year. Budgets come from the presentations to council. The Province’s open data.

      What “spent” leaves out

      An accounting charge for the future cost of closing the landfill and other assets ($1.31M in 2025). The audited statements report it; it isn’t money spent that year.

      How sure these figures are

      Money figures come from statements audited every year (BDO to 2013, KPMG since) and filed with the Province over the treasurer’s signature; they match the audited statements where we hold both. Staff counts are the township’s own, not audited. Each year’s return.

      If a number looks wrong

      Figures are copied, not calculated by the township for this page. Report it and it will be checked against the source.
